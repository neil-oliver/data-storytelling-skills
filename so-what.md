# So what

- If you can't state metric, cause, impact, and next action, you have no insight — don't ship the chart.
- Distill the visual to one sentence carrying a viewpoint and a core claim.
- State the so-what explicitly; never make the reader extract the point.
- Judge a chart by how fast the audience grasps your point, not by data completeness.
- Never present a change without its consequence (revenue, churn, risk, cost).
- Never show a bare metric; anchor it to a benchmark, target, trend, or prior period.
- Choose an anchor: prior period, goal progress, peer rank, benchmark, or historical average.
- If it isn't obvious whether a value is good, bad, up, or down, context is missing.
- Quantify the gap ("3x higher than forecast") rather than leaving readers to eyeball it.
- When a target matters, plot each value against it, not the value alone.
- When few events drive most of a total, surface that share as the headline stat.
- Distinguish signal from noise before highlighting any point as meaningful.
- Overlapping confidence intervals signal no significant difference; don't over-read.
- Flag multiple distribution peaks as mixed subpopulations worth splitting out.
- Watch for equal averages masking outliers, skew, bimodality, or high variance.
- Establish baseline volatility; never frame normal fluctuation as a trend.
- Treat an isolated spike or dip that recovers as a one-off anomaly, not a trend.
- Test whether a dip is seasonal, noise, anomaly, or market-wide before reacting.
- Rule out systematic patterns before attributing a trend change to your action.
- Compare multiple time windows before concluding a trend.
- Surface external context to separate your moves from market-wide moves.
- Present both supporting and contradicting evidence to avoid a biased conclusion.
- Before emphasizing a difference, question whether it actually matters.
- Treat identical numbers as supporting several valid stories; choose one and defend it.
- Present apples-to-apples comparisons; equal-looking visuals must mean equal measures.
