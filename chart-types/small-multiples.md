# Small multiples

- Use small multiples to break complex multi-category comparisons into guided panels.
- Give every panel identical shared axes and scale; never mix scales across panels.
- State per-panel what each measures in the panel subtitle.
- Order panels meaningfully (by value, time, or group).
- Keep color associations consistent across panels.
- Prefer small multiples over animation for side-by-side before/after comparison.
