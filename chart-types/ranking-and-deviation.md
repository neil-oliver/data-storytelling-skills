# Ranking & deviation charts

## Ranking
- Sort bars by value to rank; keep order consistent across related charts.
- Rank categories with a horizontal bar sorted longest-to-shortest.
- Highlight the rank of interest; gray the rest.
- For long rankings, prefer dot or lollipop plots to reduce ink.
- Use a rank chart when order is the point; avoid it when value gaps are the story.

## Deviation / variance
- Use diverging bars from a zero (or reference) baseline to show over- and under-performance.
- Plot the signed difference directly when the gap between two series is the story.
- Color favorable and unfavorable deviations with intuitive opposing hues.
- Anchor deviation charts at the reference value, not at zero volume.

## Likert / survey
- For Likert scales, anchor disagree left and agree right around a shared center.
- Keep neutral as its own category, not split across the divide.
- Print percentage values inside the bars; people judge stacked lengths poorly.
