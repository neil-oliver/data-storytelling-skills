# Specialized & cyclical charts

## Radial bar & polar area
- Reserve radial bars for cyclical data like seasonal or hourly patterns.
- Use a polar area chart for magnitude across cyclical or grouped categories.
- Prefer a plain bar or line unless the cycle itself is the point.

## Hemicycle / parliament
- Map legislative seat counts to a curved hemicycle mirroring the chamber.

## Radar / spider (avoid)
- Avoid radar/spider charts; connecting lines and areas mislead across categories.
- Prefer small-multiple bars when precise multivariate comparison matters.
- Use parallel coordinates instead to spot multivariate clusters, then filter interactively.

## Attention-seeking forms
- For scroll-stopping media, a distinctive custom form can beat a plain bar.
- Keep exact value labels on stylized or illustrated marks so accuracy survives the metaphor.
- Match chart form to venue: conventional for boardrooms, distinctive for media.
